NANOG 98 Kea / Stork Workshop

Exercise 6

Simulate client traffic using perfdhcp on "client" to both kea-dhcp4 and kea-dhcp6. Learn about various reports available in the Stork UI.

  1. Send approximately 100 DHCPv4 packets simulating 100 clients.
    perfdhcp -l eth1 -4 -r 10 -R 100 -p 11
    ⎘ COPY

  1. On the Stork UI, click the refresh button next to the DHCP Dashboard title. The IPv4 subnet should appear about 40% filled with a green color. Hovering over this indicator should report 100 assigned addresses.

  2. Click "Leases Search" under the "DHCP" menu on the top left (the main menu may collapse to a hamburger menu depending on the width of your screen).

  3. Type 00:0c:01:02:03:04 in the "Search leases" box and press "Enter".

  4. Two leases should be returned (one from each Kea server). You can expand these records using the arrows to view more details about the leases.

  5. Send DHCPv6 traffic to populate similar statistics with this command which will request both an address and a prefix delegation:
    perfdhcp -l eth1 -6 -r 10 -R 100 -p 11 -e address-and-prefix -b duid=000200007ed90cc084d303000912
    ⎘ COPY

  1. Click "Dashboard" under the "DHCP" menu (Reminder: the main menu may collapse to a hamburger menu depending on your screen width) to return to the dashboard.

  2. Click the same refresh button as before next to the DHCP Dashboard title. You may have to click this button a couple of times. Not much will change here as far as the bar filling with green as there are a lot of IPv6 addresses available here. However, a hover should show around 100 "Assigned NAs" and the same for "Assigned PDs".

  3. Return to the "Leases Search" page under the "DHCP" menu.

  4. Type 00:02:00:00:7e:d9:0c:c0:00:0c:01:02:03:04 in the "Search leases" box and press enter.

  5. This time you should have four results instead of two: two IA_NA IPv6 addresses and two IA_PD prefix delegations. As before, you can expand these leases to see further details.
